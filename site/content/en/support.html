Support

Got a question? Here are the essentials.

How to use the checklist

  • Open the checklist and pick your belt.
  • Tick the box next to each technique you already know.
  • Open “Note and link” to write a note or paste a link.
  • The red bar shows how far you are from the next belt.

How your progress is saved

  • Everything is saved in your browser, on this device.
  • There is no account, so nothing syncs between devices.
  • To see the same progress elsewhere, always use the same browser.

Why your progress can disappear

  • If you clear your browser data, your progress is erased.
  • In private mode, it disappears when you close the window.
  • Some browsers clear sites you haven't visited in a long time.
  • If the browser can't save, the checklist tells you on screen.

Visit counting

  • We count visits and clicks on the store buttons anonymously, with our own server.
  • No cookies and no third-party services. Your progress still stays only on your device.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track, we count nothing.
  • Details are on the privacy page.

Contact us

  • Write to suporte@tatame0.com.
  • Tell us which device and browser you use. It helps a lot.
