Privacy

In short: what you check off and write stays on your device. That's it.

Last updated: 2026-10-30

What is saved, and where

  • Your progress, notes and links stay only in your browser.
  • They live in this device's local storage (localStorage).
  • Your progress never leaves your device: it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't use

  • There is no account or login.
  • We don't use cookies.
  • We don't use third-party trackers.

What we count

  • We count visits anonymously, with our own server.
  • Each visit records the page, the language and which site you came from (the site name only).
  • If the link had campaign tags (utm), we keep those tags.
  • We also count clicks on the App Store and Google Play buttons, and when we show the invitation to the app.
  • This uses no cookies and does not identify you: there is no visitor ID.
  • No third-party service receives this data.
  • The server keeps its normal access log (like any website), and nothing more.

If you don't want to be counted

  • We respect your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.
  • With either one on, the site sends no counts at all.

Deleting your data

  • If you clear your browser data, your progress is gone.
  • The same goes for private mode when you close the window.
  • We don't keep a copy, so we can't recover it.

The native apps

  • The iPhone and Android apps are separate from this site.
  • They also keep your data on the device itself.
