Privacy

In short: what you check off and write stays on your device. That's it.

Last updated: 2026-10-30

What is saved, and where

  • Your progress, notes and links stay only in your browser.
  • They live in this device's local storage (localStorage).
  • None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't use

  • There is no account or login.
  • We don't use cookies.
  • We don't use third-party trackers.
  • Today we don't measure visits at all.

If we ever measure visits

  • If we ever measure visits, it will be anonymous counts, with no cookies.
  • When that happens, we will update this page.

Deleting your data

  • If you clear your browser data, your progress is gone.
  • The same goes for private mode when you close the window.
  • We don't keep a copy, so we can't recover it.

The native apps

  • The iPhone and Android apps are separate from this site.
  • They also keep your data on the device itself.
